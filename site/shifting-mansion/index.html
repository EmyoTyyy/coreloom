How to Play

Goal

Explore the cursed mansion, collect clues and keys, survive the nights, uncover the truth, and escape. The mansion rearranges itself every night.

Controls

  • W A S D / Arrows — move room to room through doors
  • Shift + move — sprint (faster, but noisy & frightening)
  • E — interact / pick up / read
  • I — toggle inventory focus
  • M — toggle map & help overlay
  • Space — wait (let time pass)
  • Esc — pause
  • ~ — toggle debug tools

Survival

  • Darkness drains sanity and raises fear.
  • The entity is drawn to noise, fear, and dark rooms.
  • Safe Rooms restore calm — the entity cannot enter.
  • Candles + matches widen your sight and steady your nerves.
  • Clues unlock better endings. Find 8+ important clues plus the Mirror Shard and Basement Emblem for the true ending.

The Nights

  • Each night has an objective shown in the top bar.
  • When the night ends, the mansion shifts — layout, doors and items change.
  • Your clues, story progress and carried items persist.

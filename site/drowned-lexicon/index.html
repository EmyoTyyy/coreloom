The Drowned Lexicon
A Decipherment of Tel Vaskir

The lagoon city of Tel Vaskir sank in a single night, three thousand years ago. Its archive survived in clay. Its language did not.

You have seventy-two days before the monsoon closes the site forever. No dictionary exists. No one alive has heard a word of Old Vaskiri spoken. You have a trowel, a lamp, and the assumption that the people who wrote these tablets were as reasonable as you are.
